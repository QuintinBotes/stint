# Code of conduct

Stint follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good intent, critique the work and never the person, and keep discussion on the project.

Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**, and say it is a conduct report. Reports are read only by the maintainers and handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break this code.
